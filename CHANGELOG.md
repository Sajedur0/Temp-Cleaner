## [1.0.0] — 2026-11-04

First stable release of Temp Cleaner for Windows.

- Clean temp files, Prefetch, thumbnails, crash reports, and the Recycle Bin.
- GUI review flow and scriptable command-line interface.
- Added dry-run, logging, and a 24-hour age safeguard.
- Protects system locations and skips links and in-use files.
